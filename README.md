# Auragora

*Something new. Quietly being built.*

An AI-native consumer product in stealth development.

We're exploring patterns at the intersection of AI agents, consumer product design, and mobile experience. The premise is simple: most products treat AI as a feature added to an existing pattern. We're building from a different starting point.

## What we can share

- **Stage.** Active technical buildout. Core architecture, design system, and foundational components are in place.
- **Platform.** Mobile-first. iOS to start.
- **Pace.** Solo build. Shipping every week.
- **Next.** Working toward a closed alpha.

## Working principles

**Build it first.** A working prototype tells you more than a hundred mockups.

**Taste is a system.** Design language, motion, voice, and copy all come from the same source. Inconsistency is a tax.

**Quiet beats loud.** A product that earns its presence doesn't need to shout for it.

**Agents as substrate.** AI isn't a feature bolted onto the UI. It's the medium the experience runs on.
